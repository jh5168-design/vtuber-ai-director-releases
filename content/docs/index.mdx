title: Overview
description: A local-first live director and speech-tracked teleprompter for character-driven chatting streams. You perform; the AI directs.
---

VTuber AI Director listens while you stream, follows your place in prepared dialogue, and keeps the next lines ready. It recognizes when you go off script, picks the audience messages worth answering, and rewrites only what you have not said yet. The line you are reading never changes under you.

<Callout title="What it is not">
  It is not an autonomous avatar, a voice clone, or a broadcast platform. You stay the performer and decide every word that is spoken.
</Callout>

## What it does

- **Speech-aware teleprompter.** Conservative fuzzy matching moves the highlight forward only when you actually say the line.
- **Character profiles.** Speaking style, humor, example lines, and hard boundaries shape everything the director writes.
- **Separated memory.** Memories are scoped to one character and one environment, so rehearsal never leaks into a live show.
- **Live rehearsal.** Practice against simulated audience activity with the same director a live stream uses.
- **Bilibili Open Live.** Connect a real room through the official identity-code flow.
- **Local speech recognition.** Local Whisper by default; supported browser speech recognition is optional.
